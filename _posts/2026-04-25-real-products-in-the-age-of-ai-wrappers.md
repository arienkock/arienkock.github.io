## The Shift from Meta-Tools to Real Products

Right now, many people building with AI are focusing on "meta" tools. These are often products built for other developers. Most are just wrappers around AI features. These wrappers are being quickly absorbed as AI models become more powerful.

There is not enough attention on products that could have been built without AI. These projects are now possible because development is easier and costs are lower. AI should be an enabler, not just a technology you build on top of.

### The Misconception of Quality

A lot of attention is going to people making amateur mistakes. Non-developers are building products with gaping security issues, like putting backend features in the frontend. Others build on platforms like Supabase, Lovable, or Replit. They can iterate quickly, but they lack the experience to build for scale or avoid vendor lock-in.

These mistakes lead to complacency. People see these errors and think AI is bad at building quality tools. I might be in an AI adoption bubble, but I believe adoption is simply lagging. The people building real solutions to familiar problems are often quiet and not seeking attention.

### The 10x Developer

We are not far from a major wave. It has only been a few months since the November 2025 jump in AI code quality. Even if the AI bubble bursts, current tools are already enough to 10x my personal output.

Experienced developers are in the best position ever to build their backlog of ideas. I have my own list in a Google Doc. While many ideas are dated, there are a few I have started working on. Thanks to AI, I can build viable commercial products that were never possible before while balancing family life and a full-time job.
